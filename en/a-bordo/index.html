What the floating hotel is like inside

There are three floors, each with its own use: the lounge and kitchen below, the cabins in the middle, and the deck open to the river on top.

  1. 3floor

    Third floor: Open deck, outdoor shower and shade

    Chairs and sun loungers in the open for the sun and the late afternoon, and a covered area with chairs and a coffee table for those who prefer the shade. The shower is outside.

  2. 2floor

    Second floor: Four cabins and two bathrooms

    Three cabins with four beds and one with six, all air-conditioned. The two bathrooms on this floor are shared between the cabins.

    See the cabins and rates

  3. 1floor

    First floor: Lounge, kitchen and two bathrooms

    The lounge has TV, air conditioning, tables, sofas and a beer fridge stocked with drinks. It is where the group has dinner and hangs out. The kitchen prepares the meals on board.
