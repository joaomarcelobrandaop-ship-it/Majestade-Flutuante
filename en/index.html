Floating hotel in the Pantanal, departing from Aquidauana, Brazil

Sleep on the river

Majestade is a three-story floating hotel in the Pantanal, boarding at Pesqueiro Itaju in Aquidauana (Mato Grosso do Sul, Brazil). It has 18 beds in four air-conditioned cabins, and an overnight stay with breakfast starts at R$ 200 per person.

A hotel that floats

Majestade is anchored on the water and has everything a small hotel has: four air-conditioned cabins, bathrooms, a lounge, a kitchen and an open deck on the top floor.

  • 18 beds in four cabins: three with four beds and one with six.
  • Four bathrooms, outside the cabins.
  • A lounge with TV, sofas, tables and a beer fridge, where everyone gets together after sunset.
  • Breakfast included with the overnight stay. Lunch and dinner can be arranged.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a night at Majestade cost?
An overnight stay costs R$ 200 to R$ 500 per person, per night, with breakfast included. The price depends on how many people share the cabin: R$ 200 with 4 to 6 people, R$ 250 with 3, R$ 300 with 2 and R$ 500 for one person.
How many cabins and beds does Majestade have?
There are 18 beds in four cabins: three with four beds and one with six, with bunk beds and single beds.
Do the cabins have air conditioning and a bathroom?
All four cabins have air conditioning. The bathrooms are outside the cabins: there are four in total, two on the first floor and two on the second.
Are meals included?
Breakfast is included with the overnight stay. Lunch and dinner cost R$ 75 each and are arranged in advance. Drinks, ice and snacks are sold on board.
How do I get to Majestade Flutuante?
You board at Pesqueiro Itaju, in Aquidauana (Mato Grosso do Sul), about 140 km from Campo Grande. From there, the last stretch to the hotel is by boat, at a time arranged when you book.
Can I come in my own boat?
Yes. You can arrive in your own boat or hire Majestade's boats, which are kept at Pesqueiro Itaju, in Aquidauana.
How do I book?
