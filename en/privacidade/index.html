Privacy policy

What this website does with your data, in plain words. Updated on October 6, 2026.

Who is responsible for your data

The responsible party is Majestade Flutuante, a floating hotel boarding at Pesqueiro Itaju, in Aquidauana (Mato Grosso do Sul, Brazil): the “controller” under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD). For anything about your data, message us on WhatsApp at +55 67 9206-3535.

The website was built and is maintained by João Marcelo Brandão, who runs the hosting on Majestade's behalf (the “processor”).

What the website stores

Nothing. This website has no sign-up and no forms, uses no cookies and does not measure visits. It stores none of your data.

When you message us on WhatsApp

The WhatsApp buttons only open the app with a message already written: nothing goes through the website. What you send goes straight to Majestade's WhatsApp and follows WhatsApp's own rules. Majestade uses the conversation only to reply and arrange your booking.

Hosting logs

As with any website, the company that hosts this one (Vercel) receives your device's internet address (IP), the page requested and the browser type, to deliver the page and protect itself from attacks. These logs stay with the hosting company, for its own retention period, and Majestade does not use them to identify anyone.

Your rights

Under the LGPD, you can ask whether Majestade holds any data about you, see it, correct or delete it, and withdraw consent at any time. Just ask on WhatsApp at +55 67 9206-3535. If you believe the law was not followed, you can file a complaint with Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD), at gov.br/anpd.

If this policy changes

If the website starts doing something different with data, this text changes first, and the date at the top changes with it.
