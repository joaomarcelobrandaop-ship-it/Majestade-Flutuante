Events and parties on the floating hotel, in the middle of the Pantanal

Majestade also hosts parties and group events for up to 16 people. The top-floor deck becomes the venue, with the river and the forest all around, and anyone who wants to can sleep on board.

What the floating hotel has for your group

  • Deck on the third floor, with an open part (chairs, sun loungers and an outdoor shower) and a covered part.
  • Lounge with TV, air conditioning, tables and sofas on the first floor, with a beer fridge.
  • Kitchen on board: lunch and dinner at R$ 75 each, arranged in advance, plus the snacks and drinks on the menu.
  • 18 beds in four cabins with air conditioning, for those who sleep on board.
  • Arrival by boat: 23 km along the river from Pesqueiro Itaju, in Aquidauana (Mato Grosso do Sul), about 45 minutes.

Questions about events

What kind of event can be held on Majestade?
For a celebration different from the party in the photos, tell us your idea on WhatsApp: the reply says what can be set up on board.
How many people fit at an event?
Up to 16 people per event. Anyone who wants to can sleep on board, in the four air-conditioned cabins.
How much does an event on the floating hotel cost?
The quote depends on the number of people, the date and what is included. Two prices on this website help with the math: an overnight stay costs R$ 200 to R$ 500 per person, with breakfast, and lunch and dinner cost R$ 75 each.
How do guests get to the floating hotel?
By boat. Boarding is at Pesqueiro Itaju, in Aquidauana (Mato Grosso do Sul), about 140 km from Campo Grande. From there it is 23 km along the river, about 45 minutes, at a time arranged when you book.

For the quote, send four things: what you want to celebrate, how many people, the date and whether the group sleeps on board.
