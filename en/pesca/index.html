Fishing package in the Pantanal, staying on the floating hotel

Four nights on board and three full days of fishing, Sunday to Thursday, departing from Pesqueiro Itaju in Aquidauana (Mato Grosso do Sul, Brazil).

R$ 3.500 per person, for a group of 16

Four people per cabin and four per boat. With fewer people, more days or on a weekend, the price is recalculated.

I want the 4-night package

How the days go

  • Sunday: boarding in the late afternoon and a 45-minute ride down the river to the floating hotel.
  • Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday: three full days of fishing.
  • Thursday: departure after breakfast, boarding between 7:00 and 7:30 am.

What is included

  • Boat, motor and boat pilot: wider 7 m boats with a 40 hp motor and a swivel seat for each angler. Four anglers per boat.
  • Fuel and live bait.
  • Food: breakfast, lunch, afternoon snack and dinner, plus shared platters.
  • Drinks: beer, cachaça, soft drinks, still and sparkling water.
  • Ice and cooler box.
  • Accommodation for the four nights, in an air-conditioned cabin.

Booking and payment

A R$ 500 deposit confirms the booking. The balance can be split by Pix and must be paid in full one day before boarding.

Four anglers per boat

The boats are 7 m long and wider than usual, with a 40 hp motor. Each angler has a swivel seat, and the boat pilot, fuel and live bait are part of the package.

Fishing by day and by night

Night fishing

The fishing goes on after dark: the boats also go out at night. Times and price are arranged on WhatsApp.

Ask about night fishing

Where you sleep

On the package, there are four people per cabin, all air-conditioned. The floating hotel has an air-conditioned lounge with TV, a kitchen, Starlink internet and an open deck on the top floor.

Questions about the fishing package

How much is the fishing package?
The all-inclusive package costs R$ 3,500 per person for a group of 16, Sunday to Thursday: four nights and three full days of fishing, with boat, boat pilot, fuel, live bait, food, drinks, ice and accommodation. With fewer people, more days or on a weekend, the price is recalculated.
What is included in the package?
Boat with motor and boat pilot, fuel, live bait, breakfast, lunch, afternoon snack, dinner and shared platters, drinks (beer, cachaça, soft drinks, still and sparkling water), ice, cooler box and accommodation for the four nights.
How many people go in each boat and each cabin?
Four anglers per boat and four people per cabin. The boats are 7 m long, with a 40 hp motor and a swivel seat for each angler.
Can we come with fewer than 16 people or stay longer?
Yes. The price of R$ 3,500 per person is for a group of 16, Sunday to Thursday. With fewer people, more days or on a weekend, the price is recalculated.
How do booking and payment work?
A R$ 500 deposit confirms the booking. The balance can be split by Pix and must be paid in full one day before boarding.
Where does the boat leave from, and how long does it take?
You board at Pesqueiro Itaju, in Aquidauana (Mato Grosso do Sul), about 140 km from Campo Grande. On the package, departure is on Sunday in the late afternoon, and the ride down the river to the floating hotel takes about 45 minutes.
