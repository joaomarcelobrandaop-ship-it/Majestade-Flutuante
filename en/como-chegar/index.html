How to get to the floating hotel

You board at Pesqueiro Itaju, in Aquidauana, in the Pantanal of Mato Grosso do Sul. From there, Majestade's boats take you to the floating hotel.

1

By car to Aquidauana

Aquidauana is about 140 km from Campo Grande. When you book, we send you the location of the pesqueiro and tell you where to leave the car.

2

From the pesqueiro to the hotel, by boat

The last stretch is by water. The boarding time is arranged when you book.
